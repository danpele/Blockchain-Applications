Comparing Different Anchors

Two Tiers of Peg Quality

    Question for the room With the sample to 31 May 2026, the 95% quantile of |d| is 1,883 bp for sUSD and its median 56.3 bp. The ▶ button moves the end of the sample. With the sample extended to 23 October 2026, how does the 95% quantile of |d| of sUSD change?
    Answer

    It roughly doubles, to 3,929 bp, while the median rises only from 56.3 to 62.1 bp and the share of days at least 100 bp below the peg from 26.0% to 30.3%. The 145 added days are the collapse of sUSD to 0.09 USD: they are 6% of the sample, but they fill the upper tail. The other nine coins change much less, and the two tiers keep the same members.

    A Persistent Depeg: sUSD

      Question for the room After September 2025 sUSD first closed below 0.95 USD on 24 November 2025, and it has stayed below 0.95 USD since 14 December 2025. The ▶ button lowers the threshold price. From which date does sUSD stay below 0.50 USD without interruption?
      Answer

      From 20 August 2026; it first closed below 0.50 on 12 June 2026, and on 23 October 2026 it closed at 0.09 USD. The fall from 0.95 to 0.50 took about nine months, with returns above each level before it was left for good. No arbitrage at par closes this gap, because redemption at 1 USD is not available: the depeg is a slow default, not a weekend dislocation.

      Quantile Regression and the Lasso

      The Check Loss

        Question for the room With n = 20 and τ = 0.05 the total check loss is flat between −120 and −35 bp. The ▶ button raises τ. At τ = 0.10, which values of q minimise the total loss?
        Answer

        Every q from −35 to −12 bp, the second and third smallest observations: n τ = 2 is again an integer, so the loss is flat between two observations (total loss 148.10). Whenever 20 τ is an integer the minimiser is an interval; for other τ it is one observation (τ = 0.06: q = −35 bp). For τ = 0.5 the interval is [2, 3] bp, around the median of 2 bp quoted on the slide. In a 90-day window n τ = 4.5, so the 5% quantile is unique, but still set by five days.

        Sensitivity of the 5% Quantile to Extreme Days

          Question for the room With W = 90 days the −285 bp close of USDC on 11 March 2023 moves the 5% quantile of the next day only from −1.90 to −2.15 bp. The ▶ button shortens the window. From which window length W does that close itself become the 5% quantile on 12 March 2023?
          Answer

          From W = 20 days down. The 5% quantile of W values is the value of rank ⌊0.05 (W − 1)⌋ + 1 from the bottom; it is the lowest value only while 0.05 (W − 1) < 1, that is W ≤ 20. With W = 21 to 30 it is the second lowest of the window to 11 March (−5.21 bp, the close of 10 March), and from 15 March the second SVB close, −79.3 bp. A short window gives a quantile that jumps with single days; a long window gives one that ignores how deep the worst day was.

          Worked Example: the Quantile Lasso Equation for USDC after SVB

            Question for the room The ▶ button lowers λ from λmax = 164.1 along the grid of 25 values. Does the number of active predictors grow at every step?
            Answer

            No. It goes 0, 1, 2, then back to 1 (at λ = 51.9 USDT leaves and only DAI is active), 2 up to λ = 5.2, then 3, 4, 5, 10, 12, 11, 12, 12 and 13. The Lasso path of a quantile regression is not monotone: a predictor can leave when others enter. At the selected λ̂ = 0.241 twelve of the 13 are active; the one left out is USDT, which is active at the neighbouring λ values.

            The De-pegging Pressure Index

            The DPI, April 2020 – October 2026

              Question for the room With the trend sample to 31 May 2026 the slope of ln DPI is +0.046 a year (p = 0.21). The ▶ button moves the end of the trend sample. With the sample ending on 31 December 2025, is a zero slope rejected at 5%?
              Answer

              Yes: +0.088 a year, HAC(90) standard error 0.032, p = 0.01, 95% interval [0.026, 0.151]. Adding the first five months of 2026, when the DPI was lower (annual median 0.32 against 0.52 in 2025), brings the slope to +0.046 and the p-value to 0.21; to 23 October 2026 it is +0.035 (p = 0.25). The test result depends on where the sample ends, which is one more reason to read the DPI in regimes, not as a trend.

              Contributions of Individual Coins to the DPI

                Question for the room Leaving out BUSD lowers the 2025 median of the DPI from 0.52 to 0.34. The ▶ button leaves out each coin in turn. Leaving out which other coin lowers the 2025 median the most?
                Answer

                None by more than 0.02: without EURS the 2025 median is 0.50; without PAXG 0.52, without USDC 0.52; leaving out any of the other six raises it, to between 0.53 (USDP) and 0.56 (TUSD), because the weight of BUSD then rises from 1/10 to 1/9. Leaving out sUSD gives 0.55: the late-2025 peak does not come from the sUSD depeg. Leaving out BUSD changes the whole index most (correlation of the logs 0.82; 0.96 to 0.98 for the other coins).

                Robustness of Event Responses

                  Question for the room With the post-event window of days 0..30, three own responses are significant after the Holm correction: KuCoin, the China sell-off and Terra. With a window of days 0..10, which events have a Holm p-value below 0.05?
                  Answer

                  KuCoin (+49.6%), Terra (+23.5%) and the Iran–Hormuz shock (−34.8%, Holm p = 0.049); the China sell-off is no longer significant (−12.5%, Holm p = 0.123). The window length changes which events respond, and even the sign: the Iran–Hormuz response is −35% over 10 days and +2.4% over 30. Neither window gives FTX and SVB, the two events the paper finds significant. The narrow-λ-grid index on this page is re-estimated with the notebook (+94.9% for KuCoin and +158.6% for SVB at 30 days; the lecture reports +95.3% and +158.6%).

                  Tail Dependence between Stablecoins

                  Tail Links between Stablecoins: Signed Selection Frequencies

                    Question for the room Over all days the most often aligned link is USDC ← DAI (75 pp). The ▶ button steps through the calendar years 2020 to 2026. In how many of the seven years is USDC ← DAI the most often aligned link?
                    Answer

                    In 1 of the 7, 2023, the year of the SVB weekend (91 pp). In every other year another pair leads: USDT ← BUSD and USDP ← BUSD in 2020, TUSD ← BUSD in 2021, USDP ← TUSD in 2022, BUSD ← USDT in 2024 and 2026, TUSD ← USDT in 2025. The pooled ranking averages different years; the shares of aligned (44–51%) and inverse (29–37%) coefficients are more stable than the ranking of pairs. This matrix uses the notebook's Quantile Lasso; its pooled values agree with the slide to the unit (market variables: 18.3% here, 18.4% in the lecture).

                    Model Sparsity across DPI Quintiles

                      Question for the room With quintiles of the DPI the mean active set falls from 10.96 in the lowest group to 9.68 in the highest. The ▶ button raises the number of groups from 2 to 10. With deciles, what happens to the gap between the lowest and the highest group?
                      Answer

                      It widens: 11.06 active predictors in the lowest decile against 9.51 in the highest, and the mean falls from each decile to the next. The Spearman correlation of the daily DPI with the mean active set is −0.73. Part of this is mechanical: the DPI is the mean selected λ, and a larger λ removes predictors by construction.
