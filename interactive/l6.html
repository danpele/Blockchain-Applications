Crypto Markets as Networks

Correlation Matrix and Minimum Spanning Tree (own)

    Question for the room The ▶ button builds the correlation matrix and the minimum spanning tree for each calendar year, 2021 to 2026. Which coin is the hub (the coin with the most MST edges) in most of these years?
    Answer

    Polkadot (DOT): it is the hub in 2022 (degree 5), 2023 (degree 9), 2024 (5) and 2025 (5). Litecoin is the hub in 2021 (5); in 2026, to 23 October, Bitcoin, Ether and Ethereum Classic share the highest degree (4). Over the full sample ETH and DOT tie with degree 6. The MST keeps the 18 strongest links that connect all coins, so a hub is a coin that is the nearest neighbour of many others, not the largest coin: Bitcoin has degree 1 in 2021 and 2 in 2025. The mean pairwise correlation is highest in 2022 (0.73) and lowest in 2024 (0.55).

    How Connected, and When? (own)

      Question for the room The ▶ button lengthens the window from 30 to 360 days. What happens to the highest mean pairwise correlation over all windows?
      Answer

      It falls, from 0.90 (30-day window ending 15 June 2021) to 0.85 (90 days, 14 August 2021) and 0.75 (360 days). A long window averages the days after a crash with calmer days, so the peaks of co-movement are smoothed away; the lowest value rises from 0.28 to 0.49. The average absorption ratio hardly moves, from 0.69 to 0.67: the window changes how sharply the episodes show, not the overall level of connectedness.

      Spillovers through Time (own)

        Question for the room The ▶ button raises the forecast horizon H from 1 to 20 days. How does the total spillover index change?
        Answer

        It hardly changes: for the full-sample VAR(5) it is 72.7% at H = 1, 72.9% at H = 10 and 72.9% at H = 20; in the last 200-day window it moves from 80.3% to 80.4%. The lag coefficients of daily crypto returns are small, so almost all of the forecast-error variance comes from the same-day correlation of the shocks (Σ), which is already in the H = 1 decomposition. A high spillover index here measures common same-day moves, not shocks that travel from coin to coin over the following days.

        Are Cryptos an Asset Class?

        The Map of Assets (own)

          Question for the room The ▶ button raises the weight of volatility among the six standardised features from 0 (shape only) to 1 (all six features, as on the slide). How does the number of coins in the k-means group that holds the 14 traditional assets change?
          Answer

          It falls, from 8 coins to 4. With weight 0 to 0.15 the group holds BTC, ETH, LTC, LINK, DOT, AVAX, ATOM and ALGO; from 0.2 to 0.45, seven (AVAX leaves); from 0.5 on, only BTC, ETH, LTC and LINK. All 14 traditional assets stay in the same group at every weight. Volatility separates most altcoins from traditional assets; on the shape of the returns alone, the large coins resemble equities. PC1 explains 62.7% of the variance with all six features and 64.5% with shape only.

          Event Studies: Ethereum Upgrades

          Abnormal Returns around the Event Day (own)

            Question for the room The ▶ button extends the CAR window of the 13 implementation dates from [−1, 0] to [−1, +10]. Does the mean CAR move away from zero as the window lengthens?
            Answer

            No. The mean CAR[−1, τ₂] stays between −0.25% and +1.51%, and the cross-sectional test never comes near rejection: |t| ≤ 0.53, p ≥ 0.61 for every τ₂. The individual paths fan out as the window lengthens, because each CAR adds more daily noise (residual sd 1.5–3.7% a day), but the mean does not drift. The schedule dates give the same picture: the lowest p-value is 0.17, for [−1, +4].

            Prices: EODHD daily closes to 23 October 2026; 19 coins from 2 January 2021 (2,121 daily log returns); equities, Treasuries, silver and oil funds (adjusted closes), the S&P 500, gold and four exchange rates (Monday–Friday closes, repeated closes removed). Correlations, minimum spanning trees, absorption ratios, VAR spillovers, principal components, k-means groups and event-window tests are computed in the browser from the returns, features and abnormal returns of the lecture notebook.
