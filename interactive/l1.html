What a Blockchain Records

Who Pays the Miners: Subsidy or Fees?

    Question for the room In the last 30 days of the data fees are 0.23% of miner revenue. The ▶ button applies one to four further halvings to the subsidy, with prices and fees unchanged. What fee share do the same 30 days reach after four more halvings (subsidy 0.195 BTC per block)?
    Answer

    3.61%. Four halvings divide the subsidy by 2⁴ = 16, from 53.5 to 3.3 USD million a day, while fees stay at 0.13 USD million a day; the fee share roughly doubles with each halving (0.47%, 0.93%, 1.84%, 3.61%) as long as fees are small. Even then fees do not carry the network: revenue falls from 53.7 to 3.4 USD million a day. In the 30 days to 17 January 2018, the highest fee share of the sample (17.9%), the same four halvings would give 78%.

    The Market in Numbers

    Market Value of Five Selected Coins and Bitcoin's Share

      Question for the room On 18 September 2026 Bitcoin holds 82.5% of the value of the five selected coins. What is the effective number of coins, 1/HHI, of this basket?
      Answer

      1.42. The shares are 82.5% (Bitcoin), 16.2% (Ethereum), 0.7% (Dogecoin), 0.4% (Cardano) and 0.2% (Litecoin); the HHI, the sum of the squared shares, is 0.71, and 1/0.71 = 1.42. The basket has five coins, but its value behaves like that of 1.42 coins of equal size. 1/0.825 = 1.21 would count Bitcoin alone; the Ethereum share adds 0.162² = 0.026 to the HHI.

      A Market Index: CRIX

        Question for the room The ▶ button raises the number of constituents k of the second cap-weighted index from 1 to 4. How does its tracking error against the index of all five coins change?
        Answer

        It falls from 11.13% a year (Bitcoin alone) to 2.71% (top 2), 1.69% (top 3) and 0.90% (top 4). Bitcoin dominates the basket, but its share moved between 55.8% and 88.5% since 2018, so the Bitcoin-only index still departs from the five-coin index. The Akaike information criterion chooses k = 4. The tracking error measures the difference of the daily returns, not the volatility of the index: the five-coin index itself has a volatility of 65.7% a year.

        The Rise of Stablecoins

          Question for the room From 2 April 2022 to 19 August 2023 the supply of all USD stablecoins fell from 187 to 123 USD bn. How did the supply of Tether (USDT) change between the same dates?
          Answer

          It stayed about the same: 82.1 USD bn on 2 April 2022 and 82.8 USD bn on 19 August 2023. The fall came from the other issuers: USD Coin went from 52.2 to 25.4 USD bn and Dai from 9.2 to 3.9 USD bn. The issuers do not move in proportion, and the largest issuer did not account for the largest decline.

          Who Issues the Stablecoins?

            Question for the room Between 1 April 2022 and 19 August 2023 the Tether share rose from 44.0% to 67.5% and the USD Coin share fell from 27.9% to 20.7%. What happened to the HHI of issuers?
            Answer

            It rose, from 0.282 to 0.500. Shares enter the index squared, so the largest share dominates it: 0.675² = 0.456 alone is above the April 2022 value. The two largest shares are enough to see the direction: in April 2022 the HHI could be at most 0.440² + 0.279² + 0.281² = 0.350, with all the remaining 28.1% held by a single issuer, and in August 2023 it is at least 0.456.

            DeFi: Total Value Locked

              Question for the room From the end of November 2021 to the end of September 2026, TVL fell from 174 to 88 USD bn. What does this chart allow us to conclude about the tokens deposited?
              Answer

              Not that half of the deposits left. TVL is the quantity of tokens times their price. With November 2021 as the base month, the Ether price falls to 0.56 of its level (4,632 to 2,611 USD) and TVL to 0.51 (174 to 88 USD bn). Most of the fall can come from lower prices; the chart alone cannot separate quantities from prices (the appendix of the lecture does this for a fixed-price TVL). Deposits did not rise either: TVL fell slightly more than the Ether price.

              January 2024: Spot Bitcoin Exchange-Traded Funds

                Question for the room Bitcoin and the ETF IBIT both start at 100 on 11 January 2024. Where is IBIT on 18 September 2026, compared with Bitcoin?
                Answer

                Slightly below: IBIT is at 172.8 and Bitcoin at 174.5, about 1% lower after 674 common trading days. The drift of ln(IBIT/Bitcoin), the slope of a linear trend in years, is −0.24% a year: the cost of holding Bitcoin through the fund. The fund holds the coins, so the two lines overlap, but not exactly; skipping weekends does not lift the ETF above Bitcoin, because both are compared on the same trading days.

                On-Chain Activity

                Four Views of a Blockchain

                  Question for the room With a 30-day mean, the Bitcoin fee peaks at 19.81 USD (13 January 2018). The ▶ button lengthens the window from 1 to 90 days. What happens to the highest Bitcoin fee in the fees panel?
                  Answer

                  It falls: from 92.37 USD with daily values to 28.75 USD (7 days), 19.81 USD (30 days) and 11.32 USD (90 days). A longer mean averages the peak days with calmer days, so the reported peak depends on the smoothing; the Ethereum peak falls from 37.88 to 19.39 USD. A quoted peak is only comparable with another peak smoothed in the same way.

                  Does Value Follow Activity?

                    Question for the room The ▶ button extends the sample month by month, from October 2016 to October 2025. Does the slope of the log-log line stay near the 3.6 of the full sample?
                    Answer

                    No. With the sample ending in October 2016 the slope is 0.95; it is 2.35 to October 2018, 3.28 to October 2021 and 3.59 to October 2025. The slope describes the association of two trending series over a given period; it is not a stable elasticity, and it is near the slope of 2 of Metcalfe's law only for samples ending in late 2017 or in 2018. Lecture 2 tests the relation formally.

                    Growth on a Log Scale

                    Trend Lines on a Log Scale

                      Question for the room With the sample starting in November 2020 instead of November 2014, how does the estimated growth of Bitcoin's market value change?
                      Answer

                      It falls from 79.7% to 29.6% a year (ĝ = 0.260 instead of 0.586). The regression uses every month of the sample, but the sample still decides the result: the years of fastest growth, 2015–2017, drop out. Higher market values in the later years do not mean faster growth: the slope measures the change of the log level, not the level. Transactions grow 17.1% a year from November 2020, against 10.9% from November 2014.

                      Out of Sample: the Trend One Year Later

                        Question for the room The ▶ button moves the forecast origin every three months, from October 2017 to October 2025 (33 origins). For how many origins does the realised value 12 months later lie outside the naive 95% band?
                        Answer

                        For 16 of the 33 origins (48%), every time below the band; a valid 95% band would miss about 5% of the time. The band is wide, from 0.26 to 3.8 times the forecast at the October 2025 origin, but it assumes independent residuals; the residuals have an AR(1) coefficient of 0.97, so deviations from the trend last for years and the band is far too narrow beyond a few months. At the October 2025 origin, the October 2026 value, 1,670 USD bn, lies just below the band, which starts at 1,770 USD bn.
