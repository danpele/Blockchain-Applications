Privacy notice

What the sign-in is used for

This course site uses Google sign-in for two things only: recording attendance and taking the online final exam.

What is recorded

  • your name and e-mail address, as provided by your Google account when you sign in;
  • the lectures for which you registered your attendance, with the time of registration;
  • for the exam: the questions you received, your answers, your score and the start and submission times.

The site does not read your Google password, your e-mails, your files or your contacts.

Where it is kept and who sees it

The records are kept in a spreadsheet in the Google Workspace account of the course instructor at the Bucharest University of Economic Studies (ASE). Only the course instructors can see them. They are used for attendance and grading and are not shared with anyone else.

Questions

To ask what is recorded about you, or to request a correction, write to the course instructor; the address is in the Contact section of the course page.
